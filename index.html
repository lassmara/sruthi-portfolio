Preprocessing photographed ECGs for Chat ECG

Chat ECG is an AI clinical platform: you photograph an ECG and it returns biomarkers and disease predictions from models trained on ECGs. Phone photos come out warped, so — as the preprocessing step before those models run — I trained a fully convolutional network to predict a 3D document mesh and a 2D unwarping grid for each photo and flatten it. I designed the quality-evaluation framework around grid-line straightness and SSIM, and gave an internal tech talk on the pipeline.
